## Lens: motion graphics / design critique
Look for: palette (name 4–5 colours), typography (weight, width, serif/sans, case), layout (grid, margins, safe areas, hierarchy), background treatment (flat / gradient / shader / texture / footage), depth cues (shadow, blur, parallax), transitions between scenes (what kind of cut: hard, wipe, zoom, morph), recurring motifs, text-on-screen density, what feels generic vs specific. Mark every motion claim (speed, easing, bounce) as `seen:false` unless a burst verified it — a still cannot show timing. Ask for `burst` zooms at transitions and entrances.
